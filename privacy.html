Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

Summary

音OFF does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or other user data.

Local operation

The app reads the device's current sound, vibrate, and Do Not Disturb state only to perform the action requested by the user. This processing happens entirely on the device.

Do Not Disturb access

If the optional DND step is enabled, 音OFF uses Android's Notification Policy access to manage its own app-associated DND rule. The app cannot use this permission until the user explicitly grants access in Android Settings.

Advertising and analytics

音OFF contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Data retention and sharing

Because the app does not collect user data, it retains no user data and shares no user data with the developer or third parties.

Changes

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the 音OFF GitHub issue tracker.
